---

اَلسَّلاَمُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ  
*Assalāmu’alaikum waroḥmatullōhi wabarokātuh*

---

### I. DECREE OF PANGERSA ABAH SEPUH
**Syeikh H. Abdullah Mubarok bin Nur Muhammad (PPKN 1)**

All disciples of Thoriqoh Qodiriyyah Naqsyabandiyah (TQN) Suryalaya Sirnarasa are urged to continuously strengthen bonds of brotherhood, purify their hearts through the remembrance (*dhikr*) of *Lā ilāha illallāh*, and abide strictly by the principles of TANBIH in social, religious, and civic life.

1. Preserve proper adab and reverence toward the Spiritual Guide (*Guru Mursyid*) and sacred Thoriqoh teachings.
2. Perform Daily Amaliyah (Khatam Dhikr & Khotaman) in an orderly and steadfast manner (*istiqamah*).
3. Uphold harmony among fellow brothers/sisters and all humanity.

---

### II. GUIDANCE OF PANGERSA ABAH ANOM
**Syeikh H. Ahmad Shohibul Wafa Tajul Arifin (ra)**

Pangersa Abah Anom emphasizes spiritual and mental development through TQN Suryalaya. Dhikr serves as a fortress against transgression and a means of soul purification (*Tazkiyatun Nafs*).

> "Practice Dhikr with awareness and sincerity. Guide your family and community through noble conduct (*akhlakul karimah*) and righteous example."

---

### III. LITURGICAL AMALIYAH DIRECTIVE
**Pondok Pesantren Suryalaya Sirnarasa PPKN**

Notice to all Representatives, Board Members, and TQN Disciples worldwide:

- The monthly Manaqib of Syekh Abdul Qadir Al-Jailani (qs) is conducted solemnly according to the official schedule released by Pondok Pesantren.
- Master of Ceremony (MC), Tanbih reciters, Tawassul, and Manqobah reciters must prepare thoroughly and dress in modest Islamic attire.
- Special Hijri Month Amaliyah (such as Rajab, Sya'ban, Ramadhan, Dzulhijjah) are performed strictly according to the guidance in *Uqudul Juman*.

---
